# G-LLM Client V2.0.22

V2.0.22 improves Mermaid diagram previews and copy actions, with special handling for large diagrams that exceed the available capture viewport.

## Mermaid 图表预览与复制

- Mermaid 预览自适应对话区域，图表下方的复制操作栏更紧凑，并在复制后显示成功状态。
- 改进 Mermaid 源码、PNG 和 SVG 复制。大型 PNG 会按视口分块捕获并拼接，复制前校验完整尺寸，避免图表被裁切或压扁。
- 增加大图分块尺寸与任务串行队列的回归测试。

## English Summary

V2.0.22 improves Mermaid diagram previews and copy actions. Previews adapt to the conversation area, the action bar is more compact, and successful copies show clear feedback. Large PNG exports are captured in viewport-sized tiles and stitched at the intended dimensions, preventing oversized diagrams from being clipped or flattened. Regression tests cover tile layout and serialized copy tasks.
